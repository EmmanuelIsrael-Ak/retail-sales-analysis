# Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data from 2015–2018 to provide management with a clear view of sales performance across products, customers, regions, and shipping methods.
The analysis was completed using Microsoft Excel and Power Query, with the findings presented in PowerPoint.

## Business Problem
Management had access to individual sales transactions but lacked a simple summary of overall sales performance.

This made it difficult to quickly determine total sales, identify the highest-performing product categories and regions, understand customer segment performance, and assess sales trends over time.

This analysis was conducted to transform the available sales data into clear insights that could support better business understanding and decision-making.

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this analysis were to:

- Determine the total sales generated during the 2015–2018 period.
- Identify the highest-performing product categories.
- Analyze sales performance across regions.
- Examine sales distribution across customer segments.
- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Understand the distribution of sales across shipping methods.
- Provide actionable insights and recommendations to support management decision-making.

  ## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data analysis and reporting
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **Microsoft PowerPoint** — Presentation of findings and business insights

## Data Analysis Process

The analysis followed these key steps:

1. **Data Preparation** — Reviewed the raw sales data and identified the relevant fields required for analysis.
2. **Data Cleaning** — Used Power Query to clean and organize the dataset, ensuring the data was suitable for analysis.
3. **Data Transformation** — Applied the necessary transformations to prepare the data for analysis and reporting.
4. **Data Analysis** — Used Excel to analyze sales performance across categories, regions, customer segments, shipping methods, and time periods.
5. **Insight Generation** — Identified key sales patterns, trends, and high-performing areas.
6. **Presentation** — Presented the findings, recommendations, and expected outcomes using Microsoft PowerPoint.

## Key Findings

- **Total Sales:** $2,261,232 was generated across 4,922 unique orders, with an average sales value of $459.41 per order.
- **Top Category:** Technology generated the highest sales at $827,456, followed by Furniture at $728,377 and Office Supplies at $705,399.
- **Top Region:** The West Region recorded the highest sales at $710,220, followed by the East at $669,237, Central at $492,647, and South at $389,128.
- **Customer Segment:** The Consumer segment generated the highest sales at $1,148,061, followed by Corporate at $688,471 and Home Office at $424,701.
- **Sales Trend:** Sales increased from $466,738 in 2015 to $719,726 in 2018, showing strong growth over the four-year period.

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

- Strengthen focus on **Technology products** to sustain the strongest category performance.
- Develop targeted strategies for the **South Region** to improve its lower sales performance.
- Continue engaging the **Consumer segment** while developing strategies to increase sales from Corporate and Home Office customers.
- Use the positive sales trend as a basis for planning future sales growth and expansion.
- Review shipping methods and customer ordering patterns to identify opportunities for improved efficiency and customer service.

## Expected Outcomes

The implementation of these recommendations is expected to:

- Improve overall sales performance by focusing on high-performing product categories and customer segments.
- Identify opportunities to increase sales in underperforming regions.
- Support more targeted sales and marketing strategies.
- Improve management's ability to monitor sales performance and identify growth opportunities.
- Provide a clearer data-driven foundation for future business decisions.
